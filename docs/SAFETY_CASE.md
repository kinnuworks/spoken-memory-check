# Hazard log

Each hazard, what could cause it, what the product does about it, and what is still open.

| Hazard | Cause | Control in the product | Still open |
|---|---|---|---|
| False reassurance: real decline not flagged | Slow decline; impairment already present at enrolment; a single check is noisy | Clinic memory test continues at every screening visit; the person's screen never says "normal"; a reported change in everyday tasks goes to the physician whatever the score; the benchmark states that slow decline is mostly missed within a year | Real detection rates are unknown until a validation study |
| False alarm: stable person flagged | One bad day; noise; wrong error figure | Flag only after two low checks in a row; off days do not count; the physician reviews before anyone is told; packets per 1,000 published | Error figure from published studies, not from this check |
| Speech misheard, lowering a score | Accent, dialect, older voice, noise, microphone | Two listeners; the score is a range; too much disagreement means "not scored", never a low score; words not on the list are logged, not scored | Not measured on older or Gulf Arabic speakers |
| Hearing loss mistaken for memory loss | The check is spoken | Sound check first; a failed sound check holds the check; the packet lists hearing first among contributors | No calibrated hearing test yet |
| Someone prompts the person | Family in the room | The check asks the person to do it alone | Cannot be detected |
| Distress from a result | Seeing a low score at home | Nothing abnormal is shown in the app; results are released on a fixed schedule; flagged results are given in person | Wording not yet reviewed by the public health centre |
| Urgent change handled as routine | A stroke or delirium between checks | A very large fall since the last check sends the person to urgent assessment | A scheduled check finds it days late; not a monitoring tool |
| Wrong person's data | Shared device | Scores stay on the device in this prototype | Real enrolment needs identity at the screening visit |
| Health data leaves the country | Cloud speech | On-device listener; cloud only in a UAE region and only with approval | Approval not yet sought |
| Consent lost when capacity is lost | Monitoring designed to detect decline | Consent in four parts, each revocable; the physician decides what happens next | A named family member's role needs policy |
| Overload of physicians | Too many packets | Packets per 1,000 published; stop rule if more than half are dismissed | Physician minutes per packet not measured |
| Unfair to people with little schooling | Fixed cutoffs | Each person compared with their own baseline; our survey analysis shows a fixed cutoff would flag 48% of healthy adults with under 9 years of school | Not shown in Gulf data |
