# Data sources and the right to use them

| Source | What it is | Owner | Personal data | Right to use | Where it is used |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| NHANES 2011 to 2014 | US national health survey; spoken animal naming and word recall in adults 60 and over, with schooling and self-reported memory | US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention | De-identified public-use files | Public domain | The schooling analysis in `analysis/` and the chart in the app |
| Seeded histories | Six invented people with a year of checks and a screening record | This project | None; generated by code in `src/engine/histories.ts` | Ours | The year of checks and the physician's packet |
| Simulated people | 20,000 per setting, generated by `src/engine/simulate.ts` | This project | None | Ours | The flag benchmark |
| Word lists and animal names | Ten-word lists and accepted animal names in English and Arabic | This project | None | Ours; written for this project, not taken from any published test | The spoken check |
| On-device speech model | Open Whisper speech models (base for English, small for Arabic), run in the browser | Original weights by OpenAI (MIT licence); browser versions published on Hugging Face | None; the model is downloaded, the recording never leaves the device | MIT / Apache-2.0 | The second listener |
| A tester's own checks | Scores from checks taken in the browser | The person | Scores only, kept in that browser's storage; no audio is kept by the app | The person's own | Their own trend on their own device |

Share of evidence that is synthetic: the histories and the benchmark are entirely synthetic. The schooling analysis is real survey data. No patient data is used anywhere.

Speech is heard by two listeners. The browser's own speech service may send audio to the browser's maker under its terms; the app says so before it listens. The second listener runs on the device and sends nothing anywhere.

Not used: any licensed cognitive test (for example MoCA, MMSE, TICS, CERAD); any survey whose terms bar automated tools from its person-level data.

## SADA 2022 (Saudi TV speech)

Saudi Data and AI Authority, test split, from huggingface.co/datasets/MohamedRashad/SADA22. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Used only to measure the listeners by speaker age group and dialect (`docs/OLDER_SPEAKERS_BENCH.md`). Not redistributed, not used to train or tune anything, not shipped in the app.
