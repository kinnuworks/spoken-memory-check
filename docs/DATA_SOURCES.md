|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Seeded histories | Six invented people with a year of checks and a screening record | This project | None; generated by code in `src/engine/histories.ts` | Ours | The year of checks and the physician's packet |

| Word lists and animal names | Ten-word lists and accepted animal names in English and Arabic | This project | None | Ours; written for this project, not taken from any published test | The spoken check |
| On-device speech model | Open Whisper speech models (base for English, small for Arabic), run in the browser | Original weights by OpenAI (MIT licence); browser versions published on Hugging Face | None; the model is downloaded, the recording never leaves the device | MIT / Apache-2.0 | The second listener |
| Google Speech Commands v0.02, test set | 387 one-second recordings of single English words by many speakers | Google; Warden, "Speech Commands: A Dataset for Limited-Vocabulary Speech Recognition", arXiv 1804.03209 (2018) | Recordings of volunteers' voices, published for research | CC BY 4.0: commercial use allowed with attribution | Measuring the English listeners (`docs/HUMAN_SPEECH_BENCH.md`, `docs/TEMPLATE_BENCH.md`); not shipped |
| Arabic Speech Commands v1.0 | 720 recordings of single Arabic words (digits, yes, no) by 30 speakers | Ghandoura, Hjabo and Al Dakkak (2021), [Zenodo 4662481](https://zenodo.org/records/4662481) | Recordings of volunteers' voices, published for research | CC BY 4.0: commercial use allowed with attribution | Measuring the Arabic listeners and own-voice matching; not shipped |

| Jais-ASR | Arabic speech model from the Institute of Foundation Models, run in the lab only | IFM | None | Apache-2.0 | A lab benchmark of a possible UAE-hosted third listener (`docs/HUMAN_SPEECH_BENCH.md`); not shipped |
| Computer voices in the demos | The recorded checks and the hearing-test digits are synthetic speech, labelled as such in the app | Being remade with Microsoft Azure neural text to speech on a paid resource (`scripts/make-voice-audio.ts`, which writes `public/samples/VOICES.txt`). The current files came from the macOS system voices, whose licence does not allow publishing them, so they are replaced before submission | None; no person was recorded | Azure output on a paid resource may be used and published | The recorded check, the short recorded check and the hearing check |
| Fonts | Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Serif, Geist Mono, IBM Plex Sans Arabic, served from this site | Their designers, via Fontsource | None | SIL Open Font License 1.1 | Every screen |
| A tester's own checks | Scores from checks taken in the browser | The person | Scores only, kept in that browser's storage; no audio is kept by the app | The person's own | Their own trend on their own device |

Share of evidence that is synthetic: the histories and the benchmark are entirely synthetic. The schooling analysis is real survey data. No patient data is used anywhere.

By default speech is heard by a listener that runs on the device and sends nothing anywhere. The browser's own speech service is an opt-in second listener; it may send audio to the browser's maker under its terms, and the switch says so.

Not used: any licensed cognitive test (for example MoCA, MMSE, TICS, CERAD); any survey whose terms bar automated tools from its person-level data.

## SADA 2022 (Saudi TV speech)

Saudi Data and AI Authority and the Saudi Broadcasting Authority, test split, from huggingface.co/datasets/MohamedRashad/SADA22. CC BY-NC-SA 4.0, which does not allow commercial use. Used only for a research evaluation of the listeners by speaker age group and dialect (`docs/OLDER_SPEAKERS_BENCH.md`). Not redistributed, not used to train or tune anything, not shipped in the app, and no clip or transcript is shown anywhere. DoH's Responsible AI Standard (3.2.1.5) asks for a legal review of open data whose licence limits commercial use: a commercial pilot would repeat this measurement on licensed or locally collected recordings, or ask SDAIA for permission.

## Arabic speech model served by this site

A modified copy of onnx-community/whisper-small-ar-ONNX (from ayoubkirouane/whisper-small-ar, Apache-2.0): the decoder's token-embedding table is stored as 4-bit numbers (`scripts/shrink-arabic-model.py`). Apache-2.0 allows modified copies to be redistributed; the model card and licence go with the files.
