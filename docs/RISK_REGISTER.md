# Risk register

This register follows the risk tiers in the DoH Responsible AI Standard, section 3.3 ([RAI Standard](https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/B84FF5DD027846699C51F36C439A580F.ashx)). It extends `docs/SAFETY_CASE.md` and agrees with it. Residual risk is our own rating (low, medium or high) for the prototype as built. Owners are roles; the pilot agreement names the people.

| Hazard | Cause | Control in the product | Residual risk | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| False reassurance: real decline not flagged | Slow decline; impairment already present at enrolment; a single check is noisy | The clinic memory test continues at every screening visit. The person's screen never says "normal". A reported change in everyday tasks goes to the physician whatever the score (`src/engine/change.ts`). Two routes: two checks in a row well below baseline, and a steady fall across all checks. The benchmark states that a loss of one point per check is still caught in only 52% of simulated people within a year (`docs/LIMITS.md`) | High. Real detection rates are unknown until a validation study | Clinical lead, partner site |
| False alarm: stable person flagged | One bad day; noise; wrong error figure | Flag only when two checks in a row together sit well below baseline, or the line through all checks falls more than error explains. Off days do not count. The physician reviews before anyone is told (`src/pages/Clinician.tsx`). Simulated false alarms: 5.2% a year in stable people, both routes together (`src/engine/simulate.ts`). Pilot stops if physicians dismiss more than half of packets over three months (`docs/PILOT.md`) | Medium. The error figure comes from published studies, not from this check | Clinical lead, partner site |
| Speech misheard, lowering a score | Accent, dialect, older voice, noise, microphone | Two listeners; each word marked heard by both, one or neither; the score is a range (`src/engine/score.ts`). Too much disagreement means "not scored", never a low score. Words not on the accepted list are logged, not scored | High. The on-device listener heard 62% of real English single words and 40% of Arabic ones (`docs/HUMAN_SPEECH_BENCH.md`). Not measured on older or Gulf Arabic speakers | Product owner |
| Phone runs out of memory while the speech model runs | The model needs about 1.0 GB (English) to 1.6 GB (Arabic) more memory in a phone browser engine; the phone closes the page | Each attempt is written down first; after a crash the page stops offering the model in that language and offers the browser's recogniser or typing (`src/speech/second.ts`). Devices reporting under 4 GB are not offered it. Measured in phone engines on a laptop (`scripts/phone-check.ts`) | Medium. Not yet tried on a real phone | Product lead |
| Hearing loss read as memory loss | The check is spoken | Sound check first; a second failed sound check saves a held check, never a low one (`src/pages/Check.tsx`). Digits-in-noise hearing check with an audiology referral route (`src/engine/din.ts`, `src/pages/Hearing.tsx`). The packet lists hearing among the treatable contributors when audiometry at the visit was reduced (`src/engine/packet.ts`) | Medium. The hearing check's cut-off is provisional and its digits use a computer voice | Clinical lead, partner site |
| Distress from a result | Seeing a low score at home | In the patient version the person sees only "Check received" and their trend. After approval the app sends only an invitation to a visit; results are given in person (`src/pages/Clinician.tsx`). The demo shows scores to testers, labelled as such | Medium. Invitation wording not yet reviewed by the public health centre | Public health centre |
| Health data leaves the device or the UAE | The browser's recogniser; the cloud listener; hosting | Scoring and storage stay in the browser (`src/engine/store.ts`). On-device listener sends nothing. Cloud listener only in Azure UAE North, only with consent, only once a key is set; none is set (`src/speech/cloud.ts`, `api/speech-token.js`). The browser's recogniser is off by default and the switch that turns it on says it may send audio to the browser's maker | Medium: audio leaves the device only if the person turns the browser's recogniser on, and on phones the check cannot run without it. A pilot must remove it for patients (`docs/COMPLIANCE.md`) | Product owner |
| Bias by schooling | Fixed cutoffs | Each person is compared with their own baseline. Our analysis of a public US survey shows a fixed cutoff of 14 animals flags 48% of adults with under 9 years of school who report no memory problem, against 16% of graduates (`analysis/`) | Medium. Not shown in Gulf data | Product owner |
| Bias by dialect | Recognisers and word lists built on other speech | Gulf Arabic locale for the browser listener (`src/speech/recogniser.ts`). Each person's own baseline absorbs a steady recognition gap. Re-scoring agreement tracked by language (`src/engine/rescore.ts`). Arabic marked as a draft in the app | High. Arabic word lists not reviewed by a native Gulf speaker; no Gulf speech measured | Product owner |
| Staff drift in re-scoring | Human re-scorers change habits over time, or copy the machine | Re-scorer cannot see the machine's result. One written scoring rule for code and people (`docs/SCORING_RULES.md`). Running agreement and count gap by language (`src/engine/rescore.ts`, `src/pages/Rescore.tsx`) | Medium. The tally lives on one device; no second person checks the re-scorers | Public health centre |
| Cloud listener outage | Network or service failure | The cloud call times out after 90 seconds and returns nothing; the check is scored by the remaining listeners (`src/speech/cloud.ts`, `src/speech/session.ts`). The cloud listener is optional and off by default | Low | Product owner |
| Physician overload | Too many packets | Packets per 1,000 members published from the simulation: about 50 a year at an assumed 2% in real decline. Pilot stop rule on dismissals (`docs/PILOT.md`) | Medium. Physician minutes per packet not measured | Clinical lead, partner site |
| Urgent change handled as routine | Stroke or delirium between checks | A very large fall since the last check routes to urgent assessment (`src/engine/change.ts`) | Medium. A scheduled check finds it days late | Clinical lead, partner site |
| Consent lost when capacity is lost | Monitoring designed to detect decline | Consent in separate parts, each revocable, time-stamped (`src/engine/consent.ts`). The physician decides what happens next | Medium. The role of a named family member needs policy | Partner facility |

## Overall tier: medium (self-rated)

Reasons for medium:
- A physician decides every action. The software only prepares a packet and, after approval, an invitation to a visit.
- It does not diagnose and does not replace the clinic memory test.
- Every decision comes from a fixed rule in code that gives the same answer every time. No language model is used.
- The rule, its settings and a step trace are shown to the physician in the packet.

Reasons it could be rated higher:
- It informs a clinical decision about cognitive change, and EDE may treat it as a medical device (ASSUMPTION, `docs/INTENDED_USE.md`).
- Three hazards above carry high residual risk in the prototype: false reassurance, mishearing and dialect bias, and, only where the person turns it on, audio leaving the UAE through the browser's recogniser.

If DoH rates the system high, the RAI Standard requires a conformity assessment with DoH DDGO before and after deployment. We would ask DDGO for its view before the pilot starts.
